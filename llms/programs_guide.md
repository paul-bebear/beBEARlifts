# Built-in programs guide

## How to pick

Follow these five questions in order. Keep only matching names from your shortlist at each step. These starting shortlists cover common needs; the entries below cover all 60 programs, including specialized plans.

1. **How long have you trained?** Use the frontmatter groups to start with 1 to 3 options.
   - New lifter, under 3 months: Basic Beginner Routine, Fierce 5, r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine.
   - 3 to 12 months: GZCLP, PHUL, Planet Fitness Hypertrophy.
   - Over a year: 5/3/1: Boring But Big, TSA 9-Week Intermediate, PHAT.
2. **How many days per week can you train?** Keep the matching names from your experience group.
   - New lifter, 3 days: Basic Beginner Routine, Fierce 5, r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine.
   - 3 to 12 months, 3 days: GZCLP.
   - 3 to 12 months, 4 days: PHUL, Planet Fitness Hypertrophy.
   - Over a year, 4 days: 5/3/1: Boring But Big, TSA 9-Week Intermediate.
   - Over a year, 5 days: PHAT.
3. **How many minutes can you allow per session?** Keep names whose listed range fits your available time.
   - New lifter, 30-45 minutes: Basic Beginner Routine. Allow 45 minutes for the full listed range.
   - New lifter, 45-60 minutes: Fierce 5, r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine.
   - 3 to 12 months, 45-60 minutes: GZCLP, Planet Fitness Hypertrophy, subject to your days answer.
   - 3 to 12 months, 60-90 minutes: PHUL.
   - Over a year, 45-60 minutes: 5/3/1: Boring But Big. Its text warns lower-body sessions can take 70-80 minutes.
   - Over a year, 60-90 minutes: TSA 9-Week Intermediate, PHAT, subject to your days answer.
4. **What is your goal?** Apply this filter to the names still on your shortlist.
   - New lifter, strength: Basic Beginner Routine. Strength and muscle together: Fierce 5, r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine.
   - 3 to 12 months, strength: GZCLP. Strength and muscle together: PHUL. Muscle size: Planet Fitness Hypertrophy.
   - Over a year, strength: TSA 9-Week Intermediate. Strength and muscle together: PHAT. Muscle size: 5/3/1: Boring But Big.
5. **Do you have the equipment?** Check the selected entry and its linked source before choosing.
   - Barbell, rack, bench, and pulling equipment: Basic Beginner Routine or GZCLP, if still on your shortlist.
   - Bodyweight setup with rowing support, pull-up and dip supports, and bands: r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine.
   - Dumbbells, machines, cables, and Smith machine: Planet Fitness Hypertrophy.
   - Free weights plus the listed accessory stations: Fierce 5, PHUL, or PHAT, if still on your shortlist.
   - For 5/3/1: Boring But Big or TSA 9-Week Intermediate, check the accessory equipment in their entries too.

If your shortlist becomes empty, use the matching level entries below to start another shortlist of 1 to 3 names. Repeat days, minutes, goal, and equipment in that order. A 6-day schedule or a single-lift peaking goal needs those specialized entries. No program here lists a 2-day frequency; Mike Mentzer's Consolidated Routine omits frequency and describes weekly training.

## Reading the entries

Names, days, minutes, goals, and level groups follow each source file's frontmatter. "Muscle size" means `hypertrophy`; "strength and muscle size" means `strength_and_hypertrophy`. Minutes describe sessions, not the number of weeks in a cycle.

All current `tags` lists are empty, and the frontmatter has no equipment field. Equipment therefore comes from the source's prose and programmed exercises. Lists describe the built-in version; follow source substitutions when equipment differs. Barbell setups include plates. Main lifts are a selection of the programmed movements, not the full exercise list.

The level groups follow `age`, even when the prose gives a different experience requirement. Read "Suits" before choosing. "Skip if" states a source limitation or a mismatch with its stated schedule, equipment, or goal. Missing metadata stays marked as missing.

## New lifter (under 3 months)

### Basic Beginner Routine

- Schedule and goal: 3 days/week; 30-45 minutes; strength.
- Equipment: Barbell, rack, flat bench, and chin-up bar or lat pulldown; microplates or larger weight jumps.
- Suits: First-time or returning lifters learning six movements over a maximum of 3 months.
- Skip if: You need programmed arm or core isolation, or have outgrown its low volume.
- Main lifts: Bent Over Row, Bench Press, Squat, Chin Up, Overhead Press, Deadlift.
- Source: [Basic Beginner Routine](../programs/builtin/basicBeginner.md).

### Fierce 5

- Schedule and goal: 3 days/week; 45-60 minutes; strength and muscle size.
- Equipment: Barbell, rack, bench, dumbbells, and cables for pulldowns, face pulls, pushdowns, and crunches.
- Suits: Beginners wanting compound strength work with arm, rear-delt, calf, and ab accessories.
- Skip if: Conventional Deadlift is essential; the base plan uses Romanian Deadlift instead.
- Main lifts: Squat, Bench Press, Pendlay Row, Front Squat, Overhead Press, Romanian Deadlift.
- Source: [Fierce 5](../programs/builtin/fierce-5.md).

### Ice Cream Fitness 5x5

- Schedule and goal: 3 days/week; 90+ minutes; strength and muscle size.
- Equipment: Barbell, rack, bench, curl bar or straight bar, cables, back-extension bench, and microplates.
- Suits: Beginners wanting 5x5 compounds plus arm, trap, ab, and lower-back work.
- Skip if: You need short sessions; each workout contains 7-8 exercises.
- Main lifts: Squat, Bench Press, Bent Over Row, Deadlift, Overhead Press, Bench Press Close Grip.
- Source: [Ice Cream Fitness 5x5](../programs/builtin/ice-cream-fitness-5x5.md).

### Ivysaur 4-4-8

- Schedule and goal: 3 days/week; 60-90 minutes; strength.
- Equipment: Barbell, rack, bench, and pull-up bar or lat pulldown.
- Suits: Beginners wanting alternating heavy fours and lighter eights with frequent pressing.
- Skip if: You want direct arm, shoulder-isolation, or ab work in the base plan.
- Main lifts: Bench Press, Squat, Overhead Press, Chin Up, Deadlift, Bent Over Row.
- Source: [Ivysaur 4-4-8](../programs/builtin/ivysaur-4-4-8.md).

### Phrak's Greyskull LP

- Schedule and goal: 3 days/week; 30-45 minutes; strength.
- Equipment: Barbell, rack, bench, chin-up bar or lat pulldown, and microplates or larger weight jumps.
- Suits: Beginners wanting AMRAP last sets and dedicated pulling every session.
- Skip if: You need more than one working Deadlift set per session or programmed isolation work.
- Main lifts: Overhead Press, Chin Up, Squat, Bench Press, Bent Over Row, Deadlift.
- Source: [Phrak's Greyskull LP](../programs/builtin/phrakgreyskull.md).

### r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine

- Schedule and goal: 3 days/week; 45-60 minutes; strength and muscle size.
- Equipment: Rowing support, pull-up and dip supports, bands, and a bench or edge; rings or ab wheel for later progressions.
- Suits: Beginners wanting full-body calisthenics with progressively harder exercise variations.
- Skip if: You have no rowing support; the source requires one even for beginners.
- Main lifts: Pull-up, squat, dip, hinge, row, and push-up progressions, plus a core triplet.
- Source: [r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine](../programs/builtin/recommended-routine.md).

### Starting Strength Phase 1

- Schedule and goal: 3 days/week; 30-45 minutes; strength.
- Equipment: Barbell, rack, and flat bench.
- Suits: Your first 1-3 weeks learning the basic barbell lifts, including Deadlift every session.
- Skip if: Deadlifting every session already limits recovery; Phase 2 reduces its frequency.
- Main lifts: Squat, Bench Press, Overhead Press, Deadlift.
- Source: [Starting Strength Phase 1](../programs/builtin/ss1.md).

### Starting Strength Phase 2

- Schedule and goal: 3 days/week; 30-45 minutes; strength.
- Equipment: Barbell, rack, bench, and microplates; bumper plates and a platform are ideal for Power Clean.
- Suits: Novices completing Phase 1 who need to alternate Deadlift with Power Clean.
- Skip if: You want direct arm or ab accessories; the base plan omits them.
- Main lifts: Squat, Bench Press, Overhead Press, Deadlift, Power Clean.
- Source: [Starting Strength Phase 2](../programs/builtin/ss2.md).

### Starting Strength Phase 3

- Schedule and goal: 3 days/week; 30-45 minutes; strength.
- Equipment: Barbell, rack, bench, chin-up bar or lat pulldown, microplates, and eventually a dip belt.
- Suits: Novices completing Phases 1 and 2, with slowing upper-body progression; the prose says typically 2-4 months.
- Skip if: You have not learned the Phase 2 lifts or already need intermediate programming.
- Main lifts: Squat, Bench Press, Overhead Press, Deadlift, Chin Up, Power Clean.
- Source: [Starting Strength Phase 3](../programs/builtin/ss3.md).

## 3 to 12 months

### Arnold Split

- Schedule and goal: 6 days/week; 60-90 minutes; muscle size.
- Equipment: Barbell, dumbbells, benches, squat setup, and pull-up bar.
- Suits: Lifters with 6-12+ months who can recover from chest/back, shoulders/arms, and legs twice weekly.
- Skip if: You are a complete beginner or cannot commit to six training days.
- Main lifts: Bench Press, Chin Up, Bent Over Row, Deadlift, Overhead Press, Squat, Stiff Leg Deadlift.
- Source: [Arnold Split](../programs/builtin/arnold-split.md).

### Arnold's Golden Six

- Schedule and goal: 3 days/week; 60-90 minutes; muscle size.
