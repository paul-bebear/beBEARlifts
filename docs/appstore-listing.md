## Description

beBEAR is a free gym tracker for people who want to get bigger and stronger. You train. You eat. Your bear grows with you.

LOG YOUR WORKOUTS
- Pick a ready-made program or build your own.
- Log sets, reps and weight in a few taps.
- Beat last week: the app shows what you did last time and what to aim for now.
- Rest timer, plate calculator, warmups and personal records.
- Graphs for every lift, so you can see your progress.

FEED THE BEAR
- Log what you eat by typing it, scanning a barcode or taking a photo.
- See calories and protein for the day at a glance.
- Track your bodyweight with a weekly weigh-in.

GROW YOUR BEAR
- Every kilo you lift counts. Your bear levels up as you get stronger.
- Streaks and badges for showing up.
- Levels never go down.

MORE
- Record runs with GPS, pace and splits.
- Apple Watch app for the current workout.
- Live Activity on the lock screen during a workout.
- Apple Health: beBEAR reads your bodyweight, body fat, sleep, steps and nutrition, and saves your workouts and measurements to Health.
- Share workouts with friends.
- Cloud backup when you sign in with Apple, Google or email.

FREE
All tracking is free. AI food scans and AI workout help are free within a daily limit. No ads.

beBEAR is open source and based on Liftosaur by Anton Astashov (AGPL-3.0).

## Review notes

No account is needed to track workouts, food and runs. Cloud backup, friends and AI features need an account. Please use Sign in with Apple. Email sign-in sends a one-time code to the inbox, so it does not work as a demo path. AI features ask for consent before the first use and are limited to 3 uses a day and 10 a week. The Apple Watch app and the Live Activity show the current workout. Health data is used only inside the app and in the user's own cloud backup.
