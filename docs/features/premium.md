## Free and beBEAR+

Anything that logs is free. Anything that analyzes, uses AI or connects to another tool is beBEAR+.

beBEAR+ adds:

- **150 AI uses a week**, 30 a day. A free account gets 10 a week, 3 a day. They cover the meal photo, meal text, the nutrition label, the body-scan printout and the AI warm-up.
- **Week Insights**. The week card on the Home tab and its details sheet. Without beBEAR+ the card shows **See Week Insights**, which opens the beBEAR+ screen. See [Week Insights](/features/week-insights).
- **Muscle views**. The muscle map for a program and for a day. Without beBEAR+ it is blurred, with an **Unlock** button.
- **Trend lines**. The **Moving Average Window Size** setting on the measurements graph. Without beBEAR+ the row shows **beBEAR+** and opens the beBEAR+ screen. A window size you set before stays saved and comes back when beBEAR+ is on. See [Measurements](/features/measurements).
- **API and MCP**. API keys in **Me → API Keys**. Without beBEAR+ the screen shows **Get beBEAR+**. See [API](/features/api).

Free for everyone:

- **Plates calculator**. The plates for each side of the bar, next to the set, under the weight keyboard, and as the **Plates** column on the workout screen. See [Logging a Workout](/features/workout-screen).
- **Rest timer alerts**. The notification when rest ends, the Live Activity and Dynamic Island on iOS, and the Live Update chip on Android. See [Rest Timer](/features/rest-timer).
- **Apple Watch app**. See [Apple Watch](/features/apple-watch).
- **Graphs** for every lift and for body weight, with all your history. See [Graphs](/features/graphs).
- **Body measurements**, import and export.
- Programs and the editor, Liftoscript, logging workouts, history, equipment and sharing.

## Prices

beBEAR+ costs $6.99 a month, $39.99 a year, or $79.99 once for life. Purchases are not open yet. The beBEAR+ screen says **Purchases open soon.**

## How the app knows your plan

beBEAR+ belongs to your beBEAR account, the one you sign in to on the backup card. The app reads your plan from the server when you sign in and each time the app starts. It saves the answer on the phone, so the features stay on when you lift offline. Signing out turns them off on that phone.

Outside App Store builds, the backup card has a field for a beBEAR+ code. A code turns beBEAR+ on right away.

## See your plan

Tap **Unlock**, **See Week Insights** or **Get beBEAR+** to open the beBEAR+ screen. It lists what beBEAR+ adds and what stays free. With beBEAR+ on, the title says **beBEAR+ is on**.

**Me → Account** has a **🐻 beBEAR+** row. It says **Free plan** with **Get beBEAR+**, or **beBEAR+ is on** with **View**.
