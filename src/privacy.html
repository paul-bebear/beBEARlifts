beBEAR Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

beBEAR is a workout and nutrition tracker made by Paul, an independent developer (github.com/paul-bebear). This policy says what data beBEAR handles, where it goes and what you can do about it. Questions or requests: SUPPORT_EMAIL.

The short version

  • Your workouts, food log, measurements, photos and runs are stored on your phone.
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on a feature that needs it: cloud backup, friends and the feed, AI features or Spotify.
  • beBEAR shows no ads, sells no data and runs no tracking or analytics.
  • You can export everything, and you can ask us to delete everything we hold.

Data that stays on your phone

beBEAR stores these in the app's own storage on your device: workouts and programs, your food log and goals, body measurements and body scans, progress photos, run routes, steps, your daily checklist, music logs, warmups, workout reports and notes about filmed sets. Videos of sets go to your Photos library, never to beBEAR's storage. If you delete the app, or remove the web app from your Home Screen, this data is deleted with it, unless you made a backup.

Apple Health

If you allow it, beBEAR reads bodyweight, body fat, waist, sleep, nutrition (calories and protein) and steps from Apple Health, and writes measurements and finished workouts to it. Health data is used only to show your progress inside the app. It is never used for advertising and never shared with anyone. Values you import become part of your workout data, so they are included in a cloud backup if you make one. You can turn Health access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Your account

An account is optional. You need one for cloud backup, friends and AI features. To sign in you give an email address, and we send a one-time code to it. We store your email address and a user id. If you use friends, we also store the handle and display name you choose.

Cloud backup

When you run a backup, beBEAR uploads one file with your workout data, food log, measurements, body scans, run routes (GPS points), goals, steps, checklist, music log, warmups, reports and small thumbnails of filmed sets. Progress photos are not included. We keep the latest backup plus one copy per weekday, so at most 8 files. Only you can read them. They stay until you delete your account.

Friends and the feed

Sharing is off by default, and you choose it per workout. A shared post contains the program and day name, when you trained, how long, the total volume and number of sets, your top sets and personal records, and how many songs you kept. Only people you have accepted as friends can see it. If you report a post, we store the reason and any note you write, and only we can read reports. If you block someone, we store that and remove the friendship.

AI features

AI features are off until you allow them, and the app asks first. When you use one, beBEAR sends only what that feature needs to an AI provider and shows you the answer:

  • Meal scan and Describe: the meal photo or the text you typed.
  • Warmup: the exercises in that day's workout.
  • Workout report: a summary of the workout, such as exercises, sets, weights and reps.
  • Setup chat: your answers and any screenshots of a plan you choose to add.

The provider is Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude (reached through Vercel's AI Gateway). Requests carry no name or email address. When we use Google's free Gemini tier, Google may use the content to improve its products. Anthropic does not train on requests sent through its API by default. We count how many AI requests each account makes per day, to enforce limits.

Food databases

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode goes to Open Food Facts and the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central to find nutrition values. Nothing else about you is sent.

Spotify

If you connect Spotify, the app reads what is playing and what you recently played, to match songs to your sets, and it can create playlists in your Spotify account when you ask. This happens directly between your phone and Spotify. Disconnect at any time in the app or in your Spotify account settings.

Who handles data for us

  • Supabase: accounts, backups, friends and the feed. Stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Vercel: hosts the web app and the small server functions for AI and food search.
  • Google and Anthropic: AI features, as described above.
  • Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central: food search.
  • Liftosaur: the iPhone app loads exercise pictures from Liftosaur's image server, which sees your IP address like any website.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Your rights

  • See and copy your data: Settings → Export everything gives you a full file at any time.
  • Correct it: edit any entry in the app.
  • Delete it: delete entries in the app, or email SUPPORT_EMAIL and we delete your account, backups, profile, posts and reports within 30 days.
  • If you live in the EU or UK, you can also object to processing or complain to your data protection authority.

We process account and backup data to provide the features you turn on (to perform our agreement with you), and AI and Health data only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Children

beBEAR is not meant for anyone under 16. If you believe a child gave us data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Backups and friends data are protected so that each account can only reach its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your email account safe, because it is how you sign in.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top. For important changes we tell you in the app first.

Source code

beBEAR is open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0 and is based on Liftosaur by Anton Astashov. You can read exactly what the app does at github.com/paul-bebear/beBEARlifts.
