beBEAR Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

beBEAR is a workout and nutrition tracker made by Paul, an independent developer (github.com/paul-bebear). This policy says what data beBEAR handles, where it goes and what you can do about it. Questions or requests: support@paulbebear.com.

The short version

  • Your workouts, food log, measurements, photos and runs are stored on your phone.
  • Your data leaves your phone only for features that need it: your account, cloud backup, friends and the feed, AI features, food search, beBEAR+ purchases and Spotify.
  • beBEAR shows no ads, sells no data and runs no tracking or analytics.
  • You can export everything, and you can delete your account and everything we hold from inside the app.

Data stored on your phone

beBEAR stores these in the app's own storage on your device: workouts and programs, your food log and goals, body measurements and body scans, progress photos, run routes, steps, your daily checklist, music logs, warmups, workout reports and notes about filmed sets. Videos of sets go to your Photos library, never to beBEAR's storage. If you delete the app, or remove the web app from your Home Screen, this data is deleted with it, unless you made a backup.

Apple Health

If you allow it, beBEAR reads bodyweight, body fat, waist, sleep, nutrition (calories and protein) and steps from Apple Health, and writes measurements and finished workouts to it. Health data is used only to show your progress inside the app. It is never used for advertising and never shared with anyone. Values you import become part of your workout data, so they are included in a cloud backup if you make one. You can turn Health access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Your account

An account is optional. You need one for cloud backup, friends and AI features. You can sign in three ways: with a one-time code we send to your email, with Apple, or with Google. Apple or Google tells us your email address. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we get the relay address Apple makes for you. We store your email address and a user id. If you use friends, we also store the handle and display name you choose.

Cloud backup

When you run a backup, beBEAR uploads one file with your workout data, food log, measurements, body scans, run routes (GPS points), goals, steps, checklist, music log, warmups, reports and small thumbnails of filmed sets. We keep the latest backup, one copy per weekday, and one copy saved before you tap "Keep this phone's data". Your progress photos are uploaded too, one file per photo, up to 200. When you delete a photo on your phone, the app deletes it from the backup at the next sync. Only you can read your backup and photos. They stay until you delete them or your account.

Friends and the feed

Sharing is off by default, and you choose it per workout. A shared post contains the program and day name, when you trained, how long, the total volume and number of sets, your top sets and personal records, and how many songs you kept. You can also share a meal. A meal post contains the meal's description, its calories and macros, and the meal photo if it has one. Only people you have accepted as friends can see your posts. If you report a post, we store the reason and any note you write, and only we can read reports. We also get each report by email. The email holds the report, your user id, your note and the reported post's text. If you block someone, we store that and remove the friendship.

AI features

AI features are off until you allow them, and the app asks first. When you use one, beBEAR sends only what that feature needs to an AI provider and shows you the answer:

  • Meal scan and Describe: the meal photo or the text you typed.
  • Label scan: the photo of a nutrition label.
  • Body scan: the photo of a body-composition scale printout or screen, which can show your weight, body fat and other body numbers.
  • Warmup: the exercises in that day's workout.
  • Workout report: a summary of the workout, such as exercises, sets, weights and reps, and your answers, such as a lift that hurt or felt off.
  • Setup chat: your answers and any screenshots of a plan you choose to add. The answers are your age, sex, height, weight, activity level, experience, goal, training days, where you train, your food goal, whether you want agility work, and a description of your current plan, which can mention injuries.

The provider is Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude. Requests to Anthropic go through Vercel's AI Gateway. beBEAR adds no name or email address to a request. Anthropic may keep requests for a limited time under its API terms, and longer if its safety checks flag them. When we use Google's free Gemini tier, Google may use the content to improve its products. Anthropic does not train on requests sent through its API by default. We count how many AI requests each account makes per day, to enforce limits.

Food databases

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode goes to Open Food Facts and the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central to find nutrition values. Nothing else about you is sent.

Purchases

beBEAR+ is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your card. On iPhone the app uses RevenueCat to check if you have beBEAR+. RevenueCat gets your user id, or a random id if you have no account, and your App Store purchases in beBEAR. We store your plan and when it ends.

Spotify

If you connect Spotify, the app reads what is playing and what you recently played, to match songs to your sets, and it can create playlists in your Spotify account when you ask. This happens directly between your phone and Spotify. Disconnect at any time in the app or in your Spotify account settings.

Who handles data for us

  • Supabase: accounts, backups, progress photos, friends, the feed and meal photos. Stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Vercel: hosts the web app, the exercise pictures and the small server functions for AI, food search, purchases, reports and account deletion. Its AI Gateway passes AI requests to Anthropic.
  • Google and Anthropic: AI features, as described above.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose it. Apple also handles App Store payments.
  • RevenueCat: checks your beBEAR+ purchases, as described above.
  • Resend: sends us the report emails, as described above.
  • Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central: food search.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Your rights

  • See and copy your data: Settings → Export everything gives you a full file at any time.
  • Correct it: edit any entry in the app.
  • Delete it: delete entries in the app, or delete your account in Settings → Cloud backup → Delete account. This removes your account, backups, progress photos, profile, friends, workout and meal posts, meal photos, reports, AI usage counts and your RevenueCat purchase record right away. Report emails we already got stay in our inbox. The data on your phone stays until you delete the app. If you can't sign in any more, email support@paulbebear.com and we delete it within 30 days.
  • If you live in the EU or UK, you can also object to processing or complain to your data protection authority.

We process account and backup data to provide the features you turn on (to perform our agreement with you), and AI and Health data only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Children

beBEAR is not meant for anyone under 16. If you believe a child gave us data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Backups and friends data are protected so that each account can only reach its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your email, Apple or Google account safe, because it is how you sign in.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top. For important changes we tell you in the app first.
